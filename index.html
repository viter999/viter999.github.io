What it does

Data sources
Open interest and volume from Binance, Bybit, Bitget, MEXC and Gate.io; volume from Kraken and Coinbase. Streamed over WebSocket and REST, with keep-alive pings and automatic reconnects.
Plain-language flow
buyers insellers insellers outbuyers out
Price change and open-interest change combined into one reading anyone can follow, with the dollar size of the move.
Whole-market radar
A strip across the top shows the biggest markets, including BTC and stock and commodity perpetuals, even when they are not in the main list.
Move vs its own history
Every move is compared with that coin's normal range, so a 2% move on a quiet large cap and on a wild small cap are not treated the same.
Alerts
Telegram alerts when a pair crosses a threshold, with cooldowns and a daily cap so the phone does not get spammed.
Storage
15-minute snapshots in SQLite, plus a separate archiver that copies finished buckets before the live database prunes them.
Self-reporting health
The screen says when a feed is stale, when the archive has gaps, and when the running build is behind its source code. A watchdog restarts it after a crash and a single-instance guard stops duplicates.
